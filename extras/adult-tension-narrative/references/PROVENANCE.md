## Extracted concept classes

The synthesis focuses on high-level mechanisms:

- direct-vs-sanitized adult-scene rendering;
- sensory grounding;
- physical continuity;
- progressive reaction continuity;
- one-beat / interaction pacing;
- character integrity;
- prose rhythm;
- anti-template cleanup.

The implementation intentionally re-expresses these mechanisms in project-specific language and binds them to the TauriTavern Native RP authority model.
